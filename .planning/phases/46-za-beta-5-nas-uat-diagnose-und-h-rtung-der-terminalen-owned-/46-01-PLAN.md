<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
| --- | --- |
| External read-only archive to extractor subprocess | Untrusted or stalled archive bytes cross a child-process pipe and must not extend scan work indefinitely. |
| Parent publication boundary to isolated verifier child | A read-only descriptor crosses to the verifier; only exact byte, digest, identity, and protocol proof authorizes publication. |
| Terminal backend error to Socket.IO and browser | Internal storage conditions must become actionable but non-sensitive operation feedback. |
| Disposable fixture setup to protected runtime | Local diagnostic data must remain below `$TMPDIR` and cannot touch NAS, data volumes, databases, or permanent stacks. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T-46-01 | Denial of service | 7z member stdout pipe | mitigate | Enforce the existing one-archive monotonic deadline through interruptible reads, terminate and reap the exact child, and retain `ArchiveReadError`. |
| T-46-02 | Tampering | Owned verifier protocol | mitigate | Retain fixed child argv, isolated interpreter, read-only FD authority, nonce/operation/attempt/ordinal echo, full SHA-256, and pre/post identity checks. |
| T-46-03 | Elevation of privilege | Owned publication | mitigate | Keep descriptor admission lock, scan-write fence, sealed staging, and terminal rejection before publication or association. |
| T-46-04 | Information disclosure | Socket and v2 failure feedback | mitigate | Map only known classes to concise safe text and never transmit paths, FDs, hashes, provider payloads, or environment values. |
| T-46-05 | Repudiation | Scan evidence fallback | mitigate | Log stable incomplete-archive classification and preserve the distinction between raw archive evidence and complete member inventory. |
| T-46-06 | Denial of service | Proof allowance | mitigate | Derive a conservative local lower-throughput budget with bounded latency and retain the finite 900-second cap and prompt no-response failure. |
</threat_model>
