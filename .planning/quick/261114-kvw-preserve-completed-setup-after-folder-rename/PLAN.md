# Preserve completed setup after direct-folder discovery changes

## Objective

An administrator who has completed setup must remain outside the setup wizard
when a direct Games child is renamed or newly discovered. The Games tab must
surface an unrecorded child as an explicit pending disposition that can be saved
as a platform mapping or exclusion.

## Steps

1. Separate completed-account/root-health recovery from exact current discovery
   parity, preserving stale historical mappings without using them as a redirect
   trigger.
2. Return newly discovered direct children through the administration projection
   as pending, safe-by-default rows and accept their complete Games-tab save.
3. Update the storage/setup architecture contract and inspect navigation, save,
   reload, and a real scan in authenticated desktop and mobile browsers.
